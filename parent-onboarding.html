The 50-Minute Formula

Our sessions run for exactly 50 minutes. We divide this time strictly to ensure every child receives personal attention while also benefiting from collaborative Islamic studies. This chart breaks down how your child's time is invested.

Key Takeaway: The critical 10-minute 1-on-1 segment guarantees customized Tajweed and reading coaching, while the 40-minute group activity builds holistic Islamic character.

Enrollment Value

We offer flexible term packages to suit your family's schedule and goals. Fees are paid at the start of each term or monthly. Review our weekly rates below to choose the best rhythm for your child's progress.

The Daily Protocol: Arrival & Pickup

Our 50-minute sessions are highly structured. Because individual lessons are conducted in order of arrival, punctuality is critical. Follow this operational flow.

⏰

1. Arrive Early

Arrive strictly 5 minutes before the start time (4 PM or 5 PM).

➡
⬇
📖

2. Queue for 1-on-1

Individual coaching begins immediately. Lessons are taken strictly in order of arrival. Late arrivals disrupt the flow.

➡
⬇
🕌

3. Group Activity

After their lesson, students participate in the 40-minute group Islamic Studies activity.

➡
⬇
🚗

4. Prompt Pickup

Collect your child exactly at the 50-minute mark. Note: Children may leave early after their 1-on-1, but full participation is encouraged.
